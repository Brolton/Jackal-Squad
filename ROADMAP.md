# Jackal Squad production roadmap

The prototype is being expanded into a three-mission cooperative campaign. Every stage ends with parser checks, focused automated tests, the full relevant regression suite, and a dedicated Git commit.

## Stage 1 — Campaign foundation

- Three-mission catalog and distinct scene entry points.
- Mission selector for solo and hosted games.
- Campaign progress in synchronized mission state.
- Safe host-authoritative transition to the next mission.

## Stage 2 — Large mission layouts

- Emerald Approach: green jungle, branching roads, bridge chokepoints, rescue camps, frontier base.
- Desert Basin: brown-gray desert, cactus fields, rocky passes, evacuation route.
- Snowbound Highlands: white snowy forests, icy passes, final headquarters.
- Each battlefield is about 1.75 times wider and seven times longer than its original layout, with additional route encounters.
- Per-level enemy, rescue, upgrade, obstacle, extraction, and base placement data.

## Stage 3 — 2D art direction and presentation

- Cohesive original painted-pixel battlefield palette and environment assets.
- Improved vehicle, infantry, structure, projectile, explosion, and pickup visuals.
- Layered terrain detail, landmarks, shadows, particles, and screen feedback.
- Redesigned main menu, briefing, HUD, and mission-complete presentation.

## Stage 4 — Gameplay polish

- Additional encounter patterns and environmental hazards.
- Hit flashes, camera feedback, destruction effects, and clearer objective guidance.
- Pause/restart/return flow and complete campaign ending.
- Cooperative scaling and balance pass for one to four players.

## Stage 5 — Release validation

- Full solo and multiplayer regression suite.
- Headless import/parser checks and Windows export.
- Updated player instructions and verified release notes.
